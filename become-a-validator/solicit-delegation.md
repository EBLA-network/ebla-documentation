# 📣 Solicit delegation

Each validator node must have a minimum total stake of 5,000 EBLA, otherwise it will not be eligible to participate in consensus. You may delegate this to your own node, or more commonly solicit delegation from the EBLA community.&#x20;

Here are a few social channels where the EBLA community congregates, &#x20;

* EBLA's [Discord server](https://discord.com/invite/gCkYC9h8S6)
* EBLA's [Telegram group](https://t.me/EBLAnetwork)
