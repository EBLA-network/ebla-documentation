# 🪙 EBLA Token

## What's EBLA's total supply?

EBLA's total supply figures are updated on [EBLA's website](https://eblanetwork.com/token/).

The initial supply is 10 billion tokens, but as outlined in our [economics](../tech-whitepaper/economic-model.md), block rewards from staking and running consensus nodes will be inflated, at a maximum of 20% annualized yield.

## What's EBLA's circulation?

EBLA's circulation is updated on [EBLA's website](https://eblanetwork.com/token/).&#x20;

## What's EBLA's unlock schedule?

You can find details about EBLA's token allocation, unlock schedules at the [EBLA Token Information](http://token.eblanetwork.com) page.

## What function does the EBLA token serve in the ecosystem?

EBLA tokens are used for 3 things,

* **Gas fees**: used to pay for transaction processing
* **Staking**: used to secure the network against Sybil attacks, staking will have a maximum 20% total annualized yield, rewarded in the form of inflated tokens in block rewards.
* **Voting**: used to vote on changes to the network.

Here are more details around the [economics](../tech-whitepaper/economic-model.md) and the [governance ](../tech-whitepaper/governance.md)of the EBLA ecosystem.

## How and when can I claim my rewards?

You may claim your rewards at EBLA's [claim site](https://claim.eblanetwork.com). Please note that, in order to be eligible to claim rewards in the form of tokens, you must pass our KYC process on the [community site](https://community.eblanetwork.com).

## Does EBLA currently have active bounties?

The EBLA Foundation is always coming up with new ways to actively engage the community at large, check out current bounties here at the [community site](https://community.eblanetwork.com).
