# 🪙 EBLA Token

## What's EBLA's total supply?

EBLA's total supply figures are updated on [EBLA's website](https://eblanetwork.com/tokenomics.html).

EBLA has a hard cap of 12 billion tokens — 5 billion preminted at genesis and 7 billion released over time as staking rewards. As outlined in our [economics](../tech-whitepaper/economic-model.md), staking yield starts at 7% and decays toward a 1% floor.

## What function does the EBLA token serve in the ecosystem?

EBLA tokens are used for 3 things,

* **Gas fees**: used to pay for transaction processing
* **Staking**: used to secure the network against Sybil attacks; staking yield starts at 7% and decays toward a 1% floor, paid as block rewards.
* **Voting**: used to vote on changes to the network.

Here are more details around the [economics](../tech-whitepaper/economic-model.md) and the [governance ](../tech-whitepaper/governance.md)of the EBLA ecosystem.

## How and when can I claim my rewards?

Staking rewards accrue continuously and can be claimed at any time, directly on-chain from your wallet — there is no KYC or sign-up. See the [Staking](../staking/) section for how to claim.
